1. Purpose

These terms set out the rights, duties and responsibilities between WATSI ("the Company") and you ("the User") regarding the mobile application CrewNote ("the Service").

2. Definitions

  1. "Service" means the app the Company provides so that you can keep and organize, on your own phone, notes about the people you look after.
  2. "Records" means the people, tags, criteria, entries, memos, photos and related data you enter in the Service.
  3. "Unlimited Access" means the paid, one-time purchase made through Google Play or the Apple App Store ("the App Store") that removes the people limit for good. It is not a subscription.

3. Publication and amendment of the terms

  1. The Company makes these terms available inside the Service (Settings → Business information) and on the Service's App Store page.

Amendments that are unfavorable to Users are announced at least 30 days in advance. The announcement stays up for at least 30 days after the effective date, shows the old and new wording side by side, and keeps the previous version available.

4. What the Service provides

  1. Keeping entries, memos and photos per person; defining your own criteria (categories, weights, buttons); exporting Records as a backup file, CSV or PDF report; locking the app with a passcode and fingerprint/face recognition; and other features the Company may add.
  2. The Company does not create accounts. There is no sign-up or login.
  3. The Company may add or change features to improve the Service. If a major paid feature is removed, the Company gives at least 30 days' advance notice in the manner set out in Section 3(3).

5. Storage of Records and responsibility

All of your Records are stored only on your own phone. The Company runs no server and does not collect, transmit or store your Records.
  1. The Company therefore cannot access or restore your Records. If Records are lost because the phone is lost, broken or reset, the app is deleted, or through your own actions, the Company cannot recover them.
  2. We recommend keeping important Records safe with the Service's backup export feature.
  3. If your phone's operating-system backup (Google or Apple) is turned on, Records may be included in your own account backup. That is between you and the operating-system provider; the Company is not involved and cannot see the contents.
  4. All rights to your Records belong to you. The Company claims no rights over them.

6. Paid features and payment

  1. The free version lets you add up to 3 people. Adding a 4th person requires Unlimited Access. The built-in sample data does not count toward the 3.
  2. Unlimited Access is paid once and never renews. The price is the local price shown in the App Store (KRW 3,000 including VAT in the Republic of Korea).
  3. Without Unlimited Access, the people and Records you already have are kept and shown as they are, and you can always view and export them. Only adding new people is limited.
  4. Payment goes through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The Company never receives, sees or stores your payment details. Refunds follow Section 9.
  5. Unlimited Access belongs to your App Store account. After changing phones or reinstalling, use "I already purchased (restore)" inside the Service with the same App Store account.

  1. The Company may change the price of Unlimited Access for legitimate reasons such as operating costs, added features, changes in App Store fee policies, or changes in prices and exchange rates.
  2. A changed price applies only to new purchases made after the change. Existing purchases remain valid at no extra cost.

8. Withdrawal (cancellation) of the purchase

Under Article 17 of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, you may withdraw from the purchase within 7 days of receiving the written details of the contract (including electronic documents such as the store's purchase receipt e-mail). If use began later than that, the 7 days run from the day use began.
  2. However, under Article 17(2)(5) of that Act, because Unlimited Access is digital content that becomes available immediately after payment, the right of withdrawal may be limited once use has begun.
  3. The Company lets you try the Service free of charge with up to 3 people before paying, and states this limitation in these terms and on the payment screen so that you know it before paying.
  4. The limitation does not apply when you could not use the Service for reasons attributable to the Company.
  5. If the Service differs from how it was described or advertised, or is not provided as agreed, you may withdraw within 3 months of receiving the Service and within 30 days of the day you knew or could have known of it, regardless of paragraphs 1 and 2.
  6. A withdrawal made in writing, such as by e-mail, takes effect on the day you send it.

12. Points that differ from the Content User Protection Guidelines

Because the Service has no sign-up and the Company does not receive your contact details, it cannot notify you or confirm consent individually.
