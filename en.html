CrewNote Privacy Policy

Effective: August 1, 2026 · Amended version effective October 5, 2026 · This is the English version of the policy for CrewNote (Korean app name: 관리인). If the two language versions ever differ, the Korean version prevails.

CrewNote ("the app") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles personal information.

1. Summary

The app does not collect any personal information from you.
  • There is no sign-up.
  • There is no server. Everything you enter in the app (names, tags, entries, memos, criteria, and so on) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • No one — including the developer — can view what you enter.
  • However, if you contact the Company by e-mail or phone with a question or a refund request, the Company receives the information you choose to give it in order to handle the request (Section 9). This information travels only by e-mail or phone, never through the app.

2. Personal information we collect

None. The app does not collect or transmit any information, including names, contact details, location, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

3. Storage of the information you enter

  • All data you enter in the app is stored only in your device's internal storage.
  • That data is deleted together with the app when you uninstall it.
  • Any notes you keep about third parties (team members, students, and so on) are entirely your own records; the developer does not access or process them.

4. Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face)

You can use your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition) to unlock the app. Biometric data is processed by the device's operating system inside the device only; the app only receives the result of the authentication (success or failure). The app never collects or stores biometric data itself.

5. Backup files

You can export your records as a backup file. How you store, share, and keep that file is entirely your own choice and responsibility; because it contains your records, please keep it somewhere safe. Backup files are never sent to the developer.

6. Payment information

When you purchase the paid feature, the payment is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). The developer does not collect payment details such as card numbers, and only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase was completed.

7. Sharing with third parties

Since the app collects no personal information, nothing is shared with third parties, handed to external processors, or transferred abroad through the app. The inquiry and refund information in Section 9 is kept in the Company's mailboxes (Naver Mail and Gabia Hiworks) and is not provided to third parties other than the bank transfer and store refund procedures needed to pay a refund.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from children.

9. Information we receive when you contact us or request a refund

Only when you contact the Company directly, or request a withdrawal, a refund or a cancellation by a minor, do we receive the following information from you. The app itself does not collect or transmit it.

  • What we receive: your e-mail address or phone number; the store you paid through, the order number, and the date, time and amount of the payment; your request and how it was handled. If you are refunded by bank transfer: bank name, account number and account holder name. If a legal representative makes the request: the representative's name, contact details and relationship to the minor.
  • Why: to answer your question, to process the withdrawal, refund or cancellation, and to resolve disputes.
  • How long: under Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, records of contracts, withdrawals and payments are kept for 5 years and records of consumer complaints or dispute handling for 3 years, then deleted without delay. Simple inquiries are deleted within 1 year of our reply.
  • You do not have to provide this information, but without it the Company cannot identify the payment to refund or pay a refund to your account.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: watsi@naver.com

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it through an app update notice or on this page.
