CrewNote Privacy Policy

Effective: August 1, 2026 · This is the English version of the policy for CrewNote (Korean app name: 관리인). If the two language versions ever differ, the Korean version prevails.

CrewNote ("the app") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles personal information.

1. Summary

The app does not collect any personal information from you.
  • There is no sign-up.
  • There is no server. Everything you enter in the app (names, tags, entries, memos, criteria, and so on) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • No one — including the developer — can view what you enter.

2. Personal information we collect

None. The app does not collect or transmit any information, including names, contact details, location, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

3. Storage of the information you enter

  • All data you enter in the app is stored only in your device's internal storage.
  • That data is deleted together with the app when you uninstall it.
  • Any notes you keep about third parties (team members, students, and so on) are entirely your own records; the developer does not access or process them.

4. Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face)

You can use your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition) to unlock the app. Biometric data is processed by the device's operating system inside the device only; the app only receives the result of the authentication (success or failure). The app never collects or stores biometric data itself.

5. Backup files

You can export your records as a backup file. How you store, share, and keep that file is entirely your own choice and responsibility; because it contains your records, please keep it somewhere safe. Backup files are never sent to the developer.

6. Payment information

When you purchase the paid feature, the payment is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). The developer does not collect payment details such as card numbers, and only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase was completed.

7. Sharing with third parties

Not applicable. Since no personal information is collected, there is no third-party sharing, no use of external data processors, and no international transfer of data.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from children.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: watsi@naver.com

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it through an app update notice or on this page.
